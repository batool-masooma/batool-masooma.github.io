A one-page static site: `index.html` plus `assets/`. No frameworks, build step or external fonts.

The four coloured dots (logo, contact box, footer) are the four nitrogen typologies: the same signature as your poster, slides and name cards.

## Before publishing
- Check every sentence once more. All facts come from your CV, publication list and the Nature Food paper.
- **One Earth / Nature Geoscience**: only titles and status are shown. Add a one-line summary and DOI after publication, not before (embargo).
- Optional: add `assets/CV_Masooma_Batool.pdf` and a "Download CV" button in the Contact section.
- Optional: add your Google Scholar link next to ORCID.
- After N2026: upload the poster to Zenodo and add it under "Talks and posters".
- The 7 countries include Japan (N2026, November 2026).

## Publish free on GitHub Pages
1. Create a free GitHub account if needed, then a **public** repository named `batool-masooma.github.io` (this gives the short address `https://batool-masooma.github.io`).
2. Upload `index.html` and the `assets` folder, keeping the folder structure.
3. Settings → Pages → "Deploy from a branch" → `main` / `(root)` → Save.
4. After a minute or two the site is live. Add the address to your Linktree, LinkedIn (Featured), email signature and CV.

## Preview locally
Open `index.html` in a browser, or run `python3 -m http.server` in this folder and visit http://localhost:8000.
